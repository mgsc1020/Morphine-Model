The herein presented PBPK model adequately describes the pharmacokinetics of morphine, morphine-6-glucuronide (M6G), and morphine-3-glucuronide (M3G) in healthy adults following intravenous administration.

In particular, it quantitatively describes the plasma concentration-time profiles of morphine and its two glucuronide metabolites across a range of intravenous bolus and infusion doses, capturing hepatic glucuronidation via UGT2B7, minor CYP3A4 metabolism, and renal elimination including active tubular secretion of M6G. Thus, the model is fit for purpose to simulate and predict morphine, M6G, and M3G plasma exposure in healthy adults following intravenous administration, and may serve as a basis for further extension, e.g. to additional populations or routes of administration.
